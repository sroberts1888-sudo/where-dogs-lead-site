Community Terms

Accepted version: 2026-10-22

Community Terms — 22 October 2026 Places and reviews you submit are shared with signed-in Where Dogs Lead users. Be respectful. Do not post unlawful, abusive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, threatening or harmful content. Do not post spam, misleading claims, or another person's private information or location without permission. Only share material you have the right to share. Use Report place, Report review or Report user to flag concerns. You can block an author to hide their attributed contributions and unblock them in Manage blocked users. Blocking is private and does not remove shared content or prevent the author from browsing. Older places without author information cannot be filtered by author. Authorised moderators may remove places/reviews and suspend posting access. Reports are private to authorised moderation personnel. Blocking or a posting suspension does not prevent safety reporting or account deletion. Your acceptance version and time, block preferences, and reporting/moderation records are stored to operate Community safety features. See Privacy & Data in Settings for privacy and account-deletion information. By choosing Accept Community Terms, you agree to these rules before posting.
